## 📊 Projects

### 1. Texas Emergency Department Visit Analysis 2025
**Tools:** Google BigQuery (SQL) · Power BI  
**Data:** Texas Health Care Information Collection (THCIC) — 854 hospitals · Q1 & Q2 2025

Analyzed statewide ED visit volume across Texas hospitals for a board-level report.
Cleaned raw Excel data with comma-formatted numbers, NULL values, and inconsistent types
in BigQuery using REGEXP_REPLACE, SAFE_CAST, COALESCE, and CASE WHEN.

**Key findings:**
- Parkland Memorial Dallas — 99,871 ED visits in H1 2025 · highest volume in Texas
- John Peter Smith Fort Worth — 64,529 visits · #2 statewide
- 28 hospitals (3.3%) reported Q2 only — identified as new or late-reporting facilities
- 13 hospitals (1.5%) reported Q1 only — identified as closed or stopped reporting
